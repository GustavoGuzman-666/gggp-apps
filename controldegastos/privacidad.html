Privacy Policy

App: Control de Gastos (Android and Windows) · Last updated: November 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Control de Gastos apps for Android and Windows ("the app", "we") handle, how it is used, where it is stored, with whom it is shared, and how you can delete it. Control de Gastos is a personal-finance tool that helps you track installment purchases, fixed monthly expenses and the amount each person owes. The app is developed and maintained by an individual developer, reachable at gggp.controldegastos@gmail.com.

1. Summary

  • The data you enter stays on your device. We run no servers and we do not receive your data.
  • If you choose to connect a cloud account, the app saves backup files in your own cloud storage so you can restore or sync your data on your other devices: Microsoft OneDrive (Android and Windows) or Google Drive (Android).
  • We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone, we show no ads, and we use no analytics or tracking tools.

3. Microsoft account and OneDrive access

Connecting OneDrive is optional. If you tap "Connect with Microsoft" in Settings → Sync, the app asks for these permissions through the Microsoft identity platform:

  • Files.ReadWrite.AppFolder — read and write files in the app's own folder in your OneDrive (Apps folder). The app cannot see, list, read or modify any of your other files or folders.
  • User.Read — read your basic profile (your email address) to show which account is connected.
  • offline_access — keep you signed in without asking for your password each time.

What the app does with them: it creates backup files (one JSON file per backup, named as you choose) in its folder in your OneDrive, containing the data described in section 2, and it reads and updates the backup you select to keep your devices in sync and to let you restore your data. Syncing only combines your data with the selected backup; replacing or deleting a backup requires your explicit double confirmation.

Use of information received from Microsoft: information received through Microsoft Graph is used only to provide the backup and sync feature that is visible in the app. It is not transferred to third parties except as necessary to provide that feature or to comply with law; it is not used for advertising; and it is not read by humans, except with your explicit consent, for security purposes, or to comply with applicable law.

4. Google account and Google Drive access (Android)

Connecting Google Drive is optional and available in the Android app. If you tap "Connect with Google" in Settings → Sync, the app asks for one permission through Google Sign-In (Google Play services):

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata — read and write files in the app's own hidden data folder in your Google Drive. The app cannot see, list, read or modify any of your other Drive files or folders. The email address shown in the app is obtained with this same permission.

What the app does with it: it creates backup files (one JSON file per backup, named as you choose) in that hidden folder, containing the data described in section 2, and it reads and updates the backup you select to keep your devices in sync and to let you restore your data. Syncing only combines your data with the selected backup; replacing or deleting a backup requires your explicit double confirmation. The hidden folder does not appear in the normal Google Drive file list.

Google API Services User Data Policy: Control de Gastos's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular, information received from Google is used only to provide the backup and sync feature that is visible in the app; it is not transferred to third parties except as necessary to provide that feature, for security purposes or to comply with law; it is not used for advertising, including retargeting or interest-based ads; it is not used to develop, improve or train generalized machine-learning models; and it is not read by humans, except with your explicit consent, for security purposes, or to comply with applicable law.

5. How we use the information

  • To calculate and display your monthly summary, installments, fixed expenses, the amount per person and your balance.
  • To save your data on your device between sessions.
  • If you connect OneDrive or Google Drive: to back up your data and synchronize it between your devices, merging changes made on different devices.

We do not use your information for any other purpose, such as advertising, profiling, credit decisions or training machine-learning models.

6. Sharing and disclosure

We do not share, sell, rent or disclose your information to third parties. The app performs only three kinds of network communication, always over HTTPS: (1) with Microsoft (sign-in and the Microsoft Graph / OneDrive service), only when you have connected your Microsoft account; (2) with Google (Google Sign-In and the Google Drive API), only when you have connected your Google account; and (3) with the free exchange-rate service open.er-api.com, only when you tap “Update from the internet” on the Foreign tab. That last request only asks for the dollar, euro and pound exchange rates: it sends none of your data, name or identifiers, although, as with any internet connection, the service can see the IP address it is reached from. Your data in OneDrive or Google Drive is subject to Microsoft's or Google's own privacy statement and security measures. We may disclose information only if required by law.

7. Storage, security and retention

  • On your device: data is kept in the app's private storage, which the operating system isolates from other apps. Protect your device with a screen lock.
  • In OneDrive or Google Drive: the backup files live in your own account and are protected by your Microsoft or Google account security.
  • Retention: your data is kept until you delete it. The developer does not keep any copy, since the data never reaches the developer.

8. Your choices and how to delete your data

  • Edit or delete entries: you can edit or delete any purchase, expense, person, card or merchant inside the app.
  • Stop syncing: in the app, go to Settings → Sync → Disconnect. This removes the sign-in from the device.
  • Delete your cloud data: delete the backups from Settings → Backups → Delete backup in the app. You can also delete them from the app's folder in your OneDrive, or in Google Drive under Settings → Manage apps → Control de Gastos → Delete hidden app data.
  • Revoke access: for Microsoft, at account.live.com/consent/Manage (personal accounts) or myapps.microsoft.com (work or school accounts); for Google, at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete local data: uninstall the app, or clear its storage in the system settings.

Because the developer does not hold your data, deleting it through the steps above removes it completely. For any request about your data you can also write to gggp.controldegastos@gmail.com.

9. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with an updated date. If the changes affect how information received from Microsoft or Google is used, the app will not apply them to data you have already provided without asking for your consent again.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy or about your data: gggp.controldegastos@gmail.com.
